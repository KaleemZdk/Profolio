★ FEATURED PROJECT

Cinephile

An AI movie & TV app: ask in plain English and get picks with real posters and a reason for each.

Built with

PythonLangGraphMCPTMDB APIFAISS
Watch demo Try it live GitHub
  • ProblemFinding something to watch means endless scrolling, and streaming suggestions rarely explain why a title fits you.
  • SolutionA LangGraph agent that understands natural-language requests, pulls live movie & TV data from TMDB through MCP tools, and ranks picks with FAISS semantic search and your favorites.
  • ResultPersonalized recommendations you can chat with — each with a reason — plus a Discover feed built from what you've liked.

What it does

  • AI chat with recommendation cards (posters, year, type)
  • Personalized Discover feed — “Because you liked One-Punch Man”
  • Trending movies & TV this week
  • Favorites and a watch list with Watching / Plan to watch / Watched
  • Reviews and searchable chat history
